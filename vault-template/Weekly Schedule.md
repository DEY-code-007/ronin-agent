# Weekly Schedule

The recurring week. The HUD, RONIN Island's "Today" tile and the daily Telegram summary read this note. Format: one `## Weekday` heading (English day names) per day, and under it bullets that start with a time or a time range, then the label. Example lines below; replace them with your own, or delete a day you don't need.

## Monday
- 09:00-12:00 Deep work
- 12:30-13:30 Lunch
- 19:00-20:30 Gym

## Tuesday
- 09:00-12:00 Deep work

## Wednesday
- 09:00-12:00 Deep work
- 19:00-20:30 Gym

## Thursday
- 09:00-12:00 Deep work

## Friday
- 09:00-12:00 Deep work
- 16:00 Weekly review

## Saturday
- 10:00-12:00 Team practice

## Sunday
- 18:00 Plan the week
